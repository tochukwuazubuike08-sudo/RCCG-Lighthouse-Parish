About Us

More than a service. A family.

At The Lighthouse, you'll find a community built around encountering God, growing in faith, and discovering purpose together. Whether you're new to church or have walked with God for years, there's a place for you here.

We gather to worship, to fellowship, to serve, and to belong — not as an audience, but as a family walking through life together.

  • Encounter God
  • Grow in faith
  • Discover purpose
  • Worship together
  • Fellowship & serve
  • Belong to a family

A Light in the Community

The Lighthouse exists to share God's love, serve the people around us, and walk with anyone who wants to grow closer to God. It's a simple calling, lived out one Sunday, one prayer, and one relationship at a time.
